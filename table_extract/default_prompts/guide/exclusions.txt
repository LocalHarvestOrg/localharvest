Other examples of documents that should return "false" include statutes/articles, meeting minutes, contracts, financial statements, or correspondence/emails.
Tables whose rows carry financial figures rather than relationships should also return "false", even when they name people or companies:
remuneration or compensation tables, share-saving, share award or incentive programme tables, and country-by-country or tax reports
listing group entities with revenue, profit, tax or employee figures.
Shareholder lists with numbers of shares or ownership percentages are relationship tables, not financial figures, so they still qualify.
